A STUDENT-LED SOCIAL IMPACT PROJECT

Making community needs easier to reach.

In mountain areas, a blocked road or a disruption in basic services can make it difficult for people to get the help they need. Pahunch Ledger is our attempt to make community needs easier to report, review and coordinate.

WHY WE STARTED THIS

A small gap we want to work on

People may report problems through different channels, but it can be difficult to see which needs have been checked and which ones still need attention. We want to make that information easier to organise.

📍

Know where help is needed

Organise reports by village, area and type of need instead of leaving information scattered.

✓

Keep a review record

Give reviewers a place to check reports and record why a report was accepted or could not be confirmed.

🤝

Follow assistance

Help coordinators keep track of what has been assigned, what is in progress and what remains unresolved.

HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps

The platform is designed around the people who report, review and coordinate.

Report a need

A reporter enters the area, the type of need and an estimate of how many people are affected.

Review the information

A reviewer checks the report and records a decision with a note and timestamp.

Track the response

A coordinator records assistance and updates its progress so remaining gaps are easier to spot.
