How prioritisation works

The prototype uses a simple experimental rule to organise demonstration reports.

Urgency points

High = 3, Medium = 2 and Low = 1. Urgency points are multiplied by 2.

Impact points

Fewer than 20 people = 1 point; 20–99 people = 2 points; 100 or more people = 3 points.

Priority score = (Urgency × 2) + Impact points

This experimental scoring method is for demonstration and does not represent an official emergency assessment or government prioritisation standard.

Review workflow

The prototype allows a user to label the review stage of each locally saved report.

  • Pending review: The report has not yet been marked as reviewed.
  • Reviewed — details recorded: A user has marked the details as reviewed.
  • Unable to verify: The details could not be verified by the reviewer.

Changing a status is a manual action. It does not independently verify a report or confirm that an authority has reviewed it.

Prototype scope and limitations

What it demonstrates

Recording essential needs, estimating impact, calculating priority scores, organising reports and tracking manual review labels.

What it does not yet provide

Shared reporting between users, live road-condition data, independent verification, official authority contact or real-time synchronisation.

Reports are stored locally in the browser used to enter them. Clearing browser data may remove saved reports. This prototype has not been validated through real-world deployment and must not be used as a substitute for official emergency services or emergency decision-making.
