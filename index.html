From community reports to coordinated response.

A community-focused prototype to organise reported needs, support human verification and help coordinators track assistance gaps.

DEMO DISCLAIMER: This prototype uses fictional sample records. It is not connected to emergency services, government systems or actual aid delivery. Location sharing is optional. Do not enter real personal or sensitive information. Demo records are saved only in this browser on this device.
